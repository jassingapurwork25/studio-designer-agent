# Research, references and assets

## Research process
- Search current references when the task depends on current products, patterns, market conventions, or technology versions.
- Prefer official product websites, documentation, published case studies, reputable design publications, and direct source material.
- Record source URL, title, publisher, publication/access date, and the exact observed pattern.
- Compare patterns by usefulness to the user's audience and task, not by visual popularity.

## Asset integrity
- Prefer user-provided brand assets, product screenshots, real team/product photos, and licensed assets.
- Do not hotlink arbitrary images or copy a competitor's logo, illustrations, or proprietary screenshots into production.
- Use placeholder assets only when clearly marked and easy to replace.
- For generated imagery, define subject, environment, composition, crop, light, color, visual style, and intended use. Avoid asking an image model to render complex UI text; build interface text as HTML.
- Use descriptive alt text for meaningful images and empty alt text for decorative images.

## Design inspiration
Extract principles such as editorial density, strong type, clear navigation, purposeful motion, restrained color, or excellent data display. Do not clone the exact site.

## Claim integrity
Never invent customer counts, revenue, ratings, testimonials, awards, partner names, university statistics, or performance results. Mark demo data as sample/demo data.
